The short version

Four families.
One flight.

Every disc belongs to a job: get distance, find the fairway, hold a line, or finish in the basket. The rim shape changes how much speed and control it wants from your throw.

Read the numbers

The anatomy
of a flight.

Flight numbers are a shorthand, not a promise. Your speed, release angle, and wind complete the equation.

HIGHLOW
RELEASEFADE
GLIDEHIGH-SPEED TURNLOW-SPEED FADE
01

Speed

How fast the disc wants to fly. Higher speed needs more arm speed to reach its line.

02

Glide

How long it stays in the air. More glide means more carry, especially at lower power.

03

Turn

How much the disc moves right for a right-handed backhand throw at full speed.

04

Fade

How strongly it hooks left as it slows down. The final signature of the flight.

Keep this close

Small
dictionary.

Hyzer
Outside edge tilted down at release. The disc naturally fades toward that edge.
Anhyzer
Outside edge tilted up. Can shape a turnover line or help a disc hold right.
Overstable
Resists turning over and finishes with a dependable fade. Wind’s favorite.
Understable
Easier to turn at speed. A friendly choice for newer players and smooth rollers.
