The short version

Three habits.
One clear sound.

Good trombone playing begins before the first note. Sit tall, let the air move freely, and make every slide change deliberate. Progress comes from small, repeatable motions.

Build your routine ↓

Your first setup

Three things
to remember.

Before you chase high notes, make your body comfortable. A relaxed setup gives your sound room to grow.

01

Breathe low

Relax your shoulders and imagine filling the space around your waist. A calm inhale makes a steady, supported phrase.

02

Buzz gently

Bring your lips together as if saying “too,” then let them vibrate. The mouthpiece should feel centered, never forced.

03

Stay balanced

Keep the instrument supported by your left hand. Your right hand should guide the slide lightly, like moving a paintbrush.

The moving part

Map the
seven positions.

The slide changes pitch by changing the length of the tubing. Start with a comfortable B♭ and move slowly between positions.

Your 10-minute loop

Small steps
sound big.

Play this sequence every day. Keep the volume comfortable and stop before your lips feel tired.

  1. 01

    Two minutes / breathe

    Inhale for four counts, exhale for four. Keep the air smooth.

  2. 02

    Three minutes / buzz

    Buzz the mouthpiece, then connect it to easy long tones.

  3. 03

    Three minutes / move

    Play slowly through positions one to seven. Make each shift quiet.

  4. 04

    Two minutes / play

    Finish with a melody you enjoy. Musicality is the point.
