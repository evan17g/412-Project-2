What your
body notices.

Use these as observations, not diagnoses. Small changes to your environment can make digital time more comfortable.

01

Eyes & focus

Long, close-up sessions can leave eyes feeling dry or tired, especially when blinking slows. Looking farther away gives your visual system a change of distance.

A kinder digital routine

Build a
soft landing.

Think in transitions, not punishment. A few repeatable cues can help your brain move between screen time and the rest of your life.

  1. 01

    Before / choose the purpose

    Name what you are opening the device to do. One clear intention makes drifting easier to notice.

  2. 02

    During / add friction

    Silence nonessential alerts, move tempting apps, and keep water or a notebook nearby.

  3. 03

    Between / move your eyes

    Stand, stretch, walk to another room, or look out a window before starting the next task.

  4. 04

    After / protect the evening

    Dim the room, lower the stimulation, and choose a screen-free ritual that signals the day is slowing down.
